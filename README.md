## About

I'm a **Bachelor of Science and Technology** student at **UFABC**, moving toward a **Data Science** specialization. My background is in **Psychology**, and it shapes how I work: before building a dashboard or a model, I ask *"what decision will this help someone make?"*

- I question whether a problem even needs ML before building a model.
- I prototype in Figma before opening VS Code.
- I think the best dashboard is the one people actually use.

## How I work

- **Data with purpose.** Every chart or model starts from the decision it should support.
- **Design matters.** UX applies to dashboards, reports and APIs too, not only to apps.
- **Rigor.** Tests, reproducibility and honest limitations. My projects document what they *don't* guarantee.
- **Ship it.** A working version in production beats a perfect notebook.
